Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how a personal, single-user integration handles health data from an Oura Ring.

Who this is

This integration is a private tool built and used by one person: Brandon McNees ("I", "me"). It connects my own Oura Ring account to my own personal AI assistant (Muse, provided by Meta) so I can review my own health data with the assistant's help.

It is not a commercial service. It has no other users, no sign-up, and no public offering. Nobody else's Oura data is collected or processed.

Contact: bm@easyrollinc.com

What data is collected

Through the Oura API, using OAuth authorization that I grant and control, the integration accesses my own Oura data, read-only:

  • Sleep data (duration, stages, timing, sleep score)
  • Readiness data (readiness score and its contributors)
  • Heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV)
  • Activity data (activity score, steps, movement)

No Oura data is written back, changed, or deleted through this integration. Access is read-only.

How the data is used

The data is used for one purpose only: my own personal health review — viewing trends, summaries, and changes in my sleep, readiness, heart rate, and activity, with help from my personal AI assistant.

The data is not used for advertising, marketing, research on other people, insurance, employment, or any commercial purpose. My health data is never sold, rented, or traded to anyone, for any reason.

Who the data is shared with

The data is shared only with the processors needed for the integration to function:

  • Oura — the source of the data, under Oura's own privacy policy and the permissions I grant in the Oura app.
  • Meta (Muse) — my personal AI assistant, which receives the data I ask it to review so it can summarize and discuss it with me.

No other third parties receive this data. There are no analytics trackers, advertisers, or data brokers involved.

Where the data is stored

Data is stored only under my own accounts and devices. OAuth access tokens are stored in a secure credential vault, not in plain text and not in this document or any public page. Health data retrieved through the API is kept only in my own personal records and assistant history, under my accounts.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only while the integration is connected and useful to me. I can end retention at any time by:

  • Revoking the integration's access in the Oura app or my Oura account settings, which stops all further data collection immediately, and/or
  • Contacting me at bm@easyrollinc.com to request deletion of stored data associated with this integration.

On revocation or a deletion request, stored OAuth tokens are removed and retained health data associated with the integration is deleted.

Security

Access is granted through Oura's OAuth flow — the integration never sees or stores my Oura password. Tokens are stored in a secure vault with access limited to this integration. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, but access is scoped to read-only permissions and a single user's data.

My rights

Because this is my own data in my own integration, I retain full control at all times: the rights to access my data, correct it at the source (Oura), export it, delete it, and revoke this integration's access. Any person who believes this policy concerns their data — it should not, as no one else's data is collected — may contact bm@easyrollinc.com.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above will change with it. Material changes to how data is collected, used, or shared would be reflected here before they take effect.

Medical disclaimer

Summaries and observations produced through this integration are informational only. They are not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. See the Terms of Service for details.
