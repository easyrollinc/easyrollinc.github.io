How a Job Moves

FROM CAD TO FIXTURE

01

Design

It starts with your CAD and a conversation. The team that quotes the job designs the fixture around your part — datums, clamps, and how the operator will actually run it.

02

Machine

Details are machined in-house, so a design question on Tuesday is a cut part on Wednesday — no handoffs, no translation losses.

03

Assemble

Fixtures are assembled, plumbed, and wired on our floor, then proven with your panel before anything ships.

04

Check

Every fixture is checked against the part it serves and its design. What arrives at your plant is ready to run, not ready to debug.
